# Medical Education App Features Research

## Professional Medical Apps Analysis

### Anki
- Spaced repetition algorithm (SM-2, variations)
- Card templates with LaTeX support
- Multimedia cards (images, audio)
- Shared deck community
- Sync across devices
- Statistics and review heat maps

### Notion (for medical students)
- Custom databases for resources
- Linked databases for cross-referencing
- Templates for note-taking
- Calendar integration
- Kanban boards for task tracking

### UWorld QBank
- High-quality question explanations
- Performance analytics with subject breakdowns
- Tutor-like explanations
- Flagging and categorization
- Notes per question

### Amboss
- Knowledge library integrated with QBank
- Smart search across content
- Clinical cases with branching scenarios
- Study recommendations based on weaknesses

### SketchyMedical
- Visual memory anchors
- Story-based learning
- High-yield concept emphasis
- Integration with Anki
